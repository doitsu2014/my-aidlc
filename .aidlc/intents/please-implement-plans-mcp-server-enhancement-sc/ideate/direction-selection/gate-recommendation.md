## Selected direction and rationale

Approve the solution direction: Option A (thin shared `application` facade, `dtech-aidlc-mcp` as a second `[[bin]]` in the same package, hand-rolled dual-era JSON-RPC over stdio with zero new crates), with ADR-001..ADR-018 in decision-record.md as the basis for implementation-design. The gate research's recommended disposition was to pause for the single AC-012 owner answer and then approve; the owner answered (accept the window), so the pause condition is resolved.

Ideate exit criteria. (1) A feasible option and real alternatives are documented: Options A-D, A-legacy and do-nothing appear in solution-options.md and trade-off-matrix.md, and recommended-direction section 3 says why each lost (Observed). (2) Trade-offs, scope and a recommended direction are stated outright: scope-boundary sections 1-7 and decision-record ADR-001..018 (Observed). (3) The owner approves the selected direction: the owner chose Option A over B and then dual-era over the stage's legacy-only recommendation (User-provided, 2026-11-27), and the artifacts record these as owner decisions, kept separate from Recommended detail (decision-record 'How to read the status line'; architecture section 9 uses accepted only for owner-decided parts). The dual-era override is applied consistently. ADR-004 is now Owner-decided, and its superseded legacy-only rationale is kept under Status. ADR-005 rule 4 defines per-request era detection. M1-M6 modern transcripts are added. -32022 data {supported, requested} and the DiscoverResult/ListToolsResult/CallToolResult required fields were checked against the saved 2026-07-28 schema. Effort is stated as 35-40 lines, about 6 tests and 0.25-0.5 day, matching what the owner was shown. A grep for leftover legacy-only wording found only intentional references to the superseded alternative. Spike evidence matches the claims: cc-dual/frames.log shows server/discover with _meta answered by an exact DiscoverResult with no top-level serverInfo, then tools/list with _meta in the same process; ts-dual-auto.log shows resultType complete, isError results and -32602 (Observed). Most items the decision-facilitator decided with evidence are technical and properly [follow-up]: per-request era rules, framing fix, error codes, Q7-Q10, Q15-Q17, N1, N4, N6, numeric caps. One item is not purely technical. AC-012's Source names the sufficiency of the current concurrency check as an owner open question. ADR-015 and scope-boundary F9 say AC-012 is met only up to the non-atomic compare-then-rename window, the owner has not answered, and scope-boundary section 5 explicitly invites tagging it [written-approval]. Under 'unanswered owner items are [written-approval]' and the paused-by-default rule for ambiguous approval, the strict disposition is D1. The cost is low. If the owner accepts the window, no artifact must change and the gate approves right after. If the owner wants a lock, it needs zero crates (std File::lock, Observed compiling on rustc 1.100.0), and the change is local to ADR-015 inside the same direction. N3 (external CI) stays [follow-up]: Analyze classed it LP with a safe default (assume none, limit platform claims), AC-013 does not require Windows or CI, and the direction does not depend on it. The F3 reading of the AC-002 scenario ('neither silently falls back') also stays [follow-up]. It is forced by AC-001 byte-identity, both binaries select the same engine, and the default is safe and reversible.

The TS client falls back only on a malformed discover probe, not on a malformed list or call result. Only the M6 exact-frame sweep guards this. DSH has not been run.

- R5 self-asserted approver label over MCP. The owner accepted it (Q6). The missing-label path is closed by Q18 refuse.
- F6 usability ceiling of fail-closed Q5 for stages with prerequisites.
- Untested platforms: Windows CRLF/.exe/path forms. Without external CI (N3 default), platform claims stay local.
- Assumptions: DSH client 2.2.0 behaves like 2.4.0, and supportedVersions listing only modern versions is a correct reading of the spec (both hosts accepted it, Observed).
- The owner decisions are not recorded in audit.jsonl, so their provenance is only as good as the conductor's record.

## Boundary statement

This approval covers the solution direction and the decisions ADR-001..ADR-018 as written in decision-record.md, the scope boundary, the architecture design and the threat model. It permits implementation-design to start.
